Data & Privacy

PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 29 September 2026 • Veermata Jijabai Technological Institute

01 // WHAT WE COLLECT

We collect exactly 10 fields when you register: Full name, VJTI Registration number, Program, Year, Branch, Primary cricket role, Batting style, Bowling style, Past experience records, and WhatsApp contact number.

02 // WHO SEES IT

Only the authorized VJTI Cricket Selection Committee and team captains. Your personal contact details (phone numbers and registration numbers) are strictly confidential and are NEVER shared or exposed on the public site.

03 // HOW IT IS USED

Details are strictly utilized for organizing trial slots, player check-in at the ground, performance evaluation, and WhatsApp updates regarding timings and shortlists.

04 // DATA RETENTION & DELETION

If you wish to withdraw your registration or request deletion of your information, you may contact the committee coordinators directly via the WhatsApp updates channel or support email.
