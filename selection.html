SELECTION PROCESS

Merit-based selection. Fair evaluation. Clear criteria. Here's exactly how we pick the VJTI cricket team.

● SELECTION TIMELINE

1

Online registration opens. Submit your details, upload photo, confirm participation. First-come basis for trial slots.

2

DAY 1 TRIALS

Saturday, October 31, 2026

Net sessions, fielding drills, and practice matches. All registered candidates participate. Preliminary assessment by coaching panel.

3

SHORTLIST ANNOUNCEMENT

Saturday, October 31, 2026 (5:00 PM)

Top 40-50 candidates shortlisted for Day 2. Names announced on-ground and via email/WhatsApp. Check your registration email.

4

DAY 2 FINAL TRIALS

Sunday, November 1, 2026

Fitness tests, selection match (20 overs), and coach interviews. Only shortlisted candidates. Final evaluation round.

5

FINAL TEAM SELECTION

Sunday, November 1, 2026 (4:00 PM)

Final 18-member squad announced. Selected players informed immediately. Official team list posted on website and social media.

🏆

PRACTICE SEASON BEGINS

From November 5, 2026

Regular nets (Mon/Wed/Fri), match simulations, fitness regime. Represent VJTI in inter-college tournaments.

● WHAT WE LOOK FOR

IN BATTERS

  • • Solid defensive technique
  • • Shot selection and placement
  • • Ability to rotate strike
  • • Temperament under pressure
  • • Running between wickets

IN BOWLERS

  • • Consistent line and length
  • • Variations (slower ball, yorker)
  • • Economy rate in pressure situations
  • • Fitness to bowl long spells
  • • Wicket-taking ability

IN WICKETKEEPERS

  • • Clean gloving (no fumbles)
  • • Quick stumpings and run-outs
  • • Vocal leadership behind stumps
  • • Batting ability (bonus)
  • • Reading the game

IN ALL PLAYERS

  • • Sharp fielding (ground + catching)
  • • Fitness and agility
  • • Team-first attitude
  • • Coachability and learning mindset
  • • Commitment to practice schedule
