Official Guidelines

RULES & REQUIREMENTS

Know what's expected. Review the trial format, gear checklist, and conduct code before you arrive.

● TRIAL FORMAT

1

NET SESSIONS

Individual batting and bowling assessments in controlled nets. Coaches evaluate technique, shot selection, line-length, and pace.

  • ▸ Batters: Face 30-40 deliveries (pace + spin)
  • ▸ Bowlers: Bowl 2 overs (12 balls) assessed on accuracy
  • ▸ Keepers: Wicket-keeping drills (stumpings, catches, dives)
2

FIELDING DRILLS

High-intensity fielding assessment covering catches, ground fielding, throws, and agility.

  • ▸ Catching under pressure (high, low, edge simulations)
  • ▸ Ground fielding & diving saves
  • ▸ Direct-hit accuracy from 20-30 yards
3

PRACTICE MATCHES

Full 20-over format with match-like intensity. Performance under pressure, game awareness, and team chemistry evaluated.

  • ▸ Leather ball, full competitive match
  • ▸ Umpires officiate (LBW, appeals, no-balls count)
  • ▸ Live scoring, match situation handling
4

FITNESS TEST (Day 2 Only)

Shortlisted candidates undergo fitness assessment. Minimum standards must be met.

  • ▸ Yo-Yo Intermittent Recovery Test Level 1
  • ▸ Sprint tests (20m, 40m)
  • ▸ Agility ladder & cone drills

● MANDATORY GEAR CHECKLIST

You MUST bring your own equipment. Trials cannot proceed without proper gear. No exceptions.

CLOTHING

  • Full whites (shirt + trousers)
  • Cricket shoes (spikes/studs)
  • Whites socks (knee-high)
  • Sunhat/cap (optional)

PROTECTIVE GEAR

  • Batting helmet (ISI marked)
  • Batting pads (leg guards)
  • Batting gloves
  • Abdominal guard (mandatory)
  • Thigh pad, arm guard (optional)

EQUIPMENT

  • Cricket bat (your own)
  • Batting inners (optional)
  • Balls provided by committee

💼 PERSONAL ITEMS

  • Water bottle (1-2 liters)
  • Towel & spare clothes
  • Sunscreen (SPF 30+)
  • College ID + Registration ID

⚠️ NO GEAR = NO PARTICIPATION

Candidates without complete protective gear will NOT be allowed to bat or keep wicket. This is for your safety and is non-negotiable.

● COMMON QUESTIONS

Can I borrow gear from the organizers? ▼

No. All candidates must bring their own complete gear. Limited spare helmets may be available but cannot be guaranteed.

What if I don't have cricket shoes? ▼

You can participate in sports shoes temporarily, but proper cricket shoes with spikes/studs are strongly recommended for better grip and performance.

Is there a dress code for spectators/parents? ▼

Spectators are allowed but must maintain decorum. Limited seating available. Casual formal attire preferred.

Can I leave after my net session? ▼

No. All candidates must stay till 5 PM on Day 1 for practice matches and fielding drills. This is part of the evaluation.
