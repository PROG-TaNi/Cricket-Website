✔ VJTI ID card, and your Registration ID (screenshot it or save your player card)

✔ Injured or unwell? Tell the committee at check-in. Don't play through it

✔ Play hard, play clean. Respect the selectors, umpires and the ground.

THREE PRIMARY ROLES

Every player specializes. Selectors evaluate candidates within their primary discipline.

All-rounder? Pick your primary role and detail your dual skillset in "Past experience".
Discipline 01

BATTER

Build the innings.

Patience, temperament, and authority at the crease. Whether opening against the new red ball or finishing under the lights.

Evaluated on: Footwork, shot selection, strike rotation.
Discipline 02

BOWLER

Control the game.

Hit your lengths, break partnerships, exploit the seam and extract turn. Dictate terms with every delivery.

Evaluated on: Accuracy, seam/spin control, pace consistency.
Discipline 03

WICKETKEEPER

Lead from behind the stumps.

Safe hands, lightning reflexes, and the sharpest cricketing brain on the park. The heartbeat of the team in the field.

Evaluated on: Glovework, anticipation, standing up to spin.

Important Guidelines

BEFORE YOU REGISTER

Eligibility and expectations for students trying out for the college team.

ELIGIBILITY

Open strictly to bona fide, currently enrolled students of Veermata Jijabai Technological Institute (VJTI) across Degree, Diploma, and M.Tech programs.

COMMITMENT

Selected players represent VJTI across university and prestigious invitational inter-collegiate tournaments. Regular practice and commitment are expected.

TRIAL PROTOCOL

All candidates must be in full whites. Attendance is recorded at check-in with your Registration ID and physical VJTI ID card.

SELECTION INTEGRITY

Selection is based purely on skill, fitness, and game temperament as evaluated by the senior selectors and sports committee.

Got Questions?

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear answers to everything you need to know about the 2026–27 trials.

Who can register?
Current VJTI students actively enrolled in Degree, Diploma, or M.Tech programs are eligible to register.
Is leather-ball experience mandatory?
Both seasoned club cricketers and raw talent with solid fundamentals are encouraged to register. Detail your cricket background in the registration form either way.
What should I bring?
Full whites (compulsory), your own cricket gear/kit if you have it (bat, pads, gloves, helmet, abdomen guard), shoes/spikes suitable for turf grass, water bottle, and your VJTI ID card.
Where are the trials held?
At the VJTI Cricket Ground, Matunga, Mumbai on Saturday 10 October and Sunday 11 October 2026.
What time should I report?
Exact slot times will be announced on this website and broadcasted on the official WhatsApp updates channel. Turn up early and warmed up.
Do I have to attend both days?
Groups and trial days are allocated by the selection committee. You will be scheduled for your initial nets slot, and shortlisted players will return for match scenarios.
Can I register after the deadline?
No. Once registrations close, the net schedules and selector sheets are locked. Register right now to guarantee your trial slot.
I registered but lost my ID. How do I get it back?
Head over to Find My ID, enter your VJTI Reg No and registered WhatsApp number, and your ID and player card will be recovered instantly.
What if it rains or the schedule changes?
Any weather-related updates or timetable revisions will be published immediately under Announcements and broadcasted to the official WhatsApp group.
How will shortlisted players be informed?
Directly on your registered WhatsApp number, followed by the official squad reveal on the public Squad page.
Who do I contact for help?
Reach out to the Sports Committee coordinators via the official WhatsApp updates channel.
